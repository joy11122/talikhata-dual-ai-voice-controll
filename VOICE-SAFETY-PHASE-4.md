# Voice Safety & AI Reliability — Phase 4\n\nImplemented: command idempotency for voice execution, success-result replay on duplicate network retries, failed-command retry support, stronger phonetic entity fallback, user-scoped entity resolution, server-side confirmation enforcement, high-value/destructive confirmation, structured AI output validation, OpenAI/OpenRouter fallback, ambiguous-entity responses, and voice audit command IDs.\n\nA repeated UI/network retry with the same commandId will not duplicate a successful financial operation. A deliberate new voice command receives a new UUID and may execute again.\n\nPhonetic fallback is bounded to 1000 tenant-owned candidates to avoid unbounded collection scans; exact/text/regex matching remains preferred.\n
